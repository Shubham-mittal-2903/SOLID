Fiber Optic Lighting — Manufacturer & Supplier in India

SOLID is a fiber optic lighting manufacturer and supplier in India, producing decorative and architectural fiber optic lighting for hotels, resorts, landscapes and commercial facades. Our side-glow (side-emitting) fiber optic cable is driven by a colour-changing RGB light engine to create sculptural, glare-free lighting features. The 0.75mm side-emitting fiber strand is bundled and shaped into custom forms — feathers, waves, trees, canopies — and lit from a remote-controlled light engine capable of static colours, colour-cycling and multi-zone control, with light engines available up to 150W across 90+ models. Because the light source sits away from the fiber run, these fiber optic light installations stay cool to the touch, are safe near water features and greenery, and need almost no on-site maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is side-glow fiber optic lighting?

Side-glow (side-emitting) fiber optic cable is a flexible strand that glows evenly along its full length when lit from one end by a remote light engine, instead of only glowing at the tip. This lets it be bent and shaped into custom decorative forms while staying lit throughout.

Where is fiber optic lighting used?

Fiber optic lighting is commonly used for hotel and resort entrance sculptures, landscape and garden art features, water feature and fountain-adjacent lighting, ceiling canopies and starlit-sky effects, and retail, hospitality or event decor.

Is fiber optic lighting safe near water?

Yes. Because the electrical light engine sits away from the fiber run, the fiber cable itself carries no electric current, making it a safe lighting option near pools, fountains and other water features.

What wattage light engine do I need?

SOLID offers 90+ light engine models up to 150W. The right wattage depends on the total fiber length and the brightness you need — share your installation size for a sizing recommendation.
